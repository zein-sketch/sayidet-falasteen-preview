WHO WE ARE

About Sayidet Falasteen

Sayidet Falasteen Foundation is a Palestinian charitable foundation working to strengthen the lives and futures of women, children, students, patients and families across Palestine. Our work combines targeted humanitarian assistance with opportunities in education, healthcare, rehabilitation and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission

To respond to pressing humanitarian needs while expanding access to education, essential care, economic opportunity and the resources that help Palestinian communities build stronger futures.

Our Vision

Palestinian communities in which individuals and families have greater opportunities to learn, receive essential care, develop their abilities and build sustainable livelihoods.

How We Work

We support people through defined initiatives and trusted delivery arrangements. Our approach brings together immediate assistance and longer-term development, with programme priorities guided by need, available resources and responsible implementation.

Our Journey

  1. 2024 onwards

    Phase 1 · Completed

    Direct support since 2024

    Our first phase supported dialysis and cancer patients, children’s rehabilitation and behavioural therapy, university students, vulnerable families and income-generating projects. These achievements established the basis for our next stage of work.

  2. 2026 – 2027

    Phase 2 · In preparation

    Building stronger capacity

    We are strengthening the team, coordination and resources behind our programmes while preparing to launch Gaza Schools of Tomorrow, beginning with one neighbourhood classroom and a long-term vision for expansion.
