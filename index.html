SAYIDET FALASTEEN FOUNDATION

Building on what
we have already begun.

Since 2024, Sayidet Falasteen Foundation has supported Palestinian women, children, students, patients and families through education, healthcare, rehabilitation, humanitarian assistance and economic opportunity. We are building on that work to create greater opportunities for the future.

OUR IMPACT SINCE 2024

A record of showing up.

Our first phase of work has delivered direct assistance to people and families across Palestine.

  • 20Elderly dialysis patients supported with transportation and medication.
  • 5Cancer patients supported with treatment-related assistance.
  • 25Children supported through behavioural therapy and rehabilitation.
  • 8Families assisted with emergency medical needs.
  • 6University students supported to continue their education.
  • 3Income-generating projects funded and established.

Our work has also supported research and professional development for women and girls in Jerusalem.

OUR NEXT CHAPTER

Gaza Schools of Tomorrow

A place to learn. A generation prepared for the future.

We are preparing neighbourhood classrooms for children whose education and daily lives have been disrupted. The first classroom is designed for 25 children aged five to seven, with a focus on learning, emotional wellbeing and belonging. Our goal is to grow towards 12 classrooms and up to 300 educational places by October 2027, subject to available resources and conditions on the ground.
