OUR IMPACT SINCE 2024

The work behind the numbers.

Our first phase delivered direct assistance across healthcare, education, rehabilitation and economic opportunity. We report these programme categories separately so visitors can understand what was supported without treating them as a single total of unique beneficiaries.

  • 20Elderly dialysis patients supported with transportation and medication.
  • 5Cancer patients supported with treatment-related assistance.
  • 25Children supported through behavioural therapy and rehabilitation.
  • 8Families supported with emergency medical assistance.
  • 6University students supported to continue their education.
  • 3Income-generating projects funded and established.

Our work has also supported 15 women researchers in Jerusalem, through the annual Jerusalem Research Conference and the research-skills training that followed it.

People and initiatives we have supported

Women’s Economic Opportunity · Nablus

Glow with Nour

In Nablus, Nour Kilani began developing perfumes and incense from home. Support through the small-business programme helped her develop products and production. Her story illustrates how a home-based enterprise can open a pathway to income for a family.

Women’s Economic Opportunity

The Kaysi Initiative

Dana Musfar and Alyaa Jumhour developed Kaysi around reusable fabric bags. Foundation support helped advance their designs and production, connecting entrepreneurship with an environmentally conscious product.

Women’s Economic Opportunity · Nablus

Mira Soap

Howeida Abu Yaqoub developed Mira Soap using natural ingredients and Palestinian olive-oil traditions. Support for the initiative helped develop its production, connecting a local craft with a small-business opportunity.

Women’s Economic Opportunity · Jerusalem

The Jerusalem Research Conference

In October 2025 we supported the annual Jerusalem Research Conference and the specialised research-skills training that followed it. Fifteen women researchers, postgraduate students and independent researchers in the social sciences and humanities, took part. The support helped them strengthen their research tools and produce new research on Jerusalem’s history, identity and present-day challenges.

Humanitarian Aid & Healthcare · Tulkarm

Healthcare and rehabilitation support

Our healthcare work has assisted people facing critical treatment needs, supported elderly dialysis patients with transportation and medication, and helped children access behavioural therapy and rehabilitation.
