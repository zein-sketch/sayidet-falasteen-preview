Gaza Schools of Tomorrow

A place to learn. A generation prepared for the future.

Gaza Schools of Tomorrow is an educational initiative being developed by Sayidet Falasteen Foundation to help children whose homes, communities and education have been disrupted regain access to a dependable place to learn, connect and grow.

Starting with one classroom

The first neighbourhood classroom is designed for 25 children aged five to seven. The programme aims to provide foundational learning alongside a safe, consistent environment that supports emotional wellbeing, confidence and belonging.

PROGRAMME UPDATE · OCTOBER 2026

  • Field teamOne administrator, two teachers and one photographer and content creator, confirmed.
  • First classroomBeing finalised for launch, with materials in preparation.

As reported in the foundation’s proposal of 16 October 2026. The classroom had not yet opened at that date.

Our educational approach

  • Stability and Confidence

    Support children’s emotional wellbeing through dependable routines, positive relationships, play and a sense of belonging.

  • Strong Foundations for Learning

    Develop literacy, numeracy, attention, curiosity and critical thinking, with pride in Palestinian identity and culture.

  • Skills for the World Ahead

    Build towards age-appropriate digital learning and, as children progress, responsible use of modern tools and AI literacy.

A vision for expansion

Our goal is to expand towards 12 classrooms and up to 300 educational places by October 2027. This is a planned goal, not a completed achievement. Expansion will depend on funding, operational readiness and conditions on the ground.
