Using this website

This website shares information about Sayidet Falasteen Foundation and its work. We aim to keep it accurate and up to date, and we correct errors when we find them. Programme figures describe completed work unless they are clearly marked as a plan or goal.

Contributions

Contributions are voluntary. They support the foundation’s approved charitable priorities, including programme delivery and the coordination and operating resources behind it. If you indicate an area of interest, our team will confirm whether your contribution can be allocated accordingly.

Receiving entity and receipts

U.S. donations are received through Miss Palestine Organization Inc., under which the foundation operates. Receipts are issued by the receiving entity. Payment arrangements are confirmed at the time of giving.

Tax

We do not give tax advice. Whether a contribution is deductible depends on your circumstances and the status of the receiving entity. Please consult your own adviser.

Errors and refunds

If you believe a contribution was made in error, contact us as soon as possible through our contact page and we will review your request.

Other websites

Links to other websites, including GoFundMe, are provided for convenience. Those websites have their own terms and policies.

Content

Text, photographs and films on this website belong to the foundation or are used with permission. Please ask before reusing them.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through our contact page.

Our Mission

To respond to pressing humanitarian needs while expanding access to education, essential care, economic opportunity and the resources that help Palestinian communities build stronger futures.
