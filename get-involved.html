SUPPORT & COLLABORATION

Help us extend our reach.

There are many ways to support Sayidet Falasteen beyond an individual donation. We welcome people and organisations who can help us develop resources, strengthen connections and expand opportunities for our programmes.

Enquire About Collaboration Support Our Mission

Ways to get involved

  • Fundraising initiatives and events
  • Donor introductions and outreach
  • Corporate and institutional partnerships
  • Palestinian diaspora and professional networks
  • Communications and campaign support
  • Relevant pro bono professional expertise

Collaborate with us

If you represent a business, foundation, professional network or institution and would like to explore a contribution or collaboration, please contact our team. We welcome conversations that align with the foundation’s humanitarian and development mission.

Volunteer in organisational support

Opportunities for volunteers currently focus on fundraising, events, outreach, communications and professional assistance. Direct services to beneficiaries are coordinated separately through authorised programme staff and approved providers.
