Privacy notice

Draft for legal review before publication. Last updated November 2026.

Who we are

Sayidet Falasteen Foundation is a Palestinian charitable foundation. U.S. donations are received through Miss Palestine Organization Inc., under which the foundation operates. This notice explains how we handle information you share through this website.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through our forms: your name and email address, and, where you provide them, your organisation, country or city, area of interest, intended contribution amount and message. We do not ask for, and ask you not to send, personal or medical details about anyone we support.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, to arrange a contribution you have asked about, to send acknowledgments and receipts, and to keep the records we are required to keep.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run this website and our email (website form messages are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit) and, once online giving is active, our payment provider, and only as needed for those purposes, or where the law requires it. Card details are handled by the payment provider and are never stored by us.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as we need them for the purpose you gave them, and for any period the law requires for financial records.

Cookies and third parties

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to deliver them. Links to GoFundMe, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and X are governed by those services’ own policies.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you have sent us, or ask us to stop contacting you, through our contact page.

Changes

We will update this notice if our practices change, and show the date of the latest version here.

Our Mission

To respond to pressing humanitarian needs while expanding access to education, essential care, economic opportunity and the resources that help Palestinian communities build stronger futures.
