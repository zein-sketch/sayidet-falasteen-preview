OUR PROGRAMMES

Support where it is needed. Opportunity where it can grow.

Our work is organised around three connected priorities: women’s economic opportunity; education and children’s futures; and humanitarian aid and healthcare. Together, they allow us to respond to immediate challenges while investing in longer-term wellbeing and opportunity.

  • 01Women’s Economic Opportunity
  • 02Education & Children’s Futures
  • 03Humanitarian Aid & Healthcare

01

Women’s Economic Opportunity

We support Palestinian women who are building livelihoods, developing skills and pursuing income-generating work. Support is adapted to the needs of each initiative rather than defined by one universal funding amount. Our previous work includes small enterprises such as Glow with Nour, the Kaysi Initiative and Mira Soap, alongside professional and research development initiatives for women in Jerusalem.

  • Entrepreneurship and income-generating projects
  • Skills and professional development
  • Research and learning opportunities

02

Education & Children’s Futures

Education support includes assistance that helps university students continue their studies, alongside initiatives focused on children’s development and access to learning. Gaza Schools of Tomorrow will extend this commitment by preparing neighbourhood classrooms for younger children whose education has been disrupted.

  • University education support
  • Children’s learning and development
  • Gaza Schools of Tomorrow

03

Humanitarian Aid & Healthcare

We provide targeted support for people facing medical or urgent humanitarian challenges. Our completed work includes assistance for elderly dialysis patients, support for cancer patients, rehabilitation and behavioural therapy for children, and emergency medical support for vulnerable families. Specific forms of assistance depend on each case and approved delivery arrangements.

  • Dialysis-related assistance
  • Cancer patient support
  • Rehabilitation and behavioural therapy
  • Emergency family assistance

Each initiative addresses a different need, but all are part of the same commitment: improving access to care, learning, dignity and opportunity across Palestinian communities.

Our Mission

To respond to pressing humanitarian needs while expanding access to education, essential care, economic opportunity and the resources that help Palestinian communities build stronger futures.
